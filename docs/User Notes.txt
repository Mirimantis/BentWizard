Add to joint templates: a toggle if a joint is Handed or not. Some joint designs may be fully symmetrical and defined from the center line. In that case applying them can be a simple rotation or mirror.

Is Square Rule necessary to enforce, or can it be an option selected during joint design? Could we have Mill Rule joints as well, or joints that are defined from a datum?

A thought about the joint design: tenons are often full-depth on their timber. Fewer cuts that have to be made if they extend directly from the shoulder. In that case, a MT joint would be able to have the same Tenon_Height as Beam Height. This design breaks due to the coincident edges in that case; Maybe it just needs to be a different joint in the library in the future.

Another note for future joint designs: Tenon_Setback_Face1 and Tenon_Setback_Face2 are not descriptive enough, and should indicate which direction (from the bottom or side). I suppose Face 1 is currently set as the bottom of a beam, but that may be editable in the future.
